Logic Owl brings Sudoku and dozens of unusual logic puzzles together in one calm, uncluttered app.

Learn a new puzzle, follow a hunch, or take your time with an old favourite. Every puzzle has one solution, reached through logic.

Get Logic Owl on Google Play ↗

Free · No ads · No in-app purchases

Something new to learn

Start with Sudoku, Nonogram or Kakuro, then explore less familiar puzzles. Each type includes a tutorial, hints and four difficulty levels.

02

Play at your own pace

Numbered puzzles, daily challenges and random puzzles give you plenty to explore. Your progress saves automatically, and you can play offline.

03

Your progress stays yours

Cloud backup is optional. On Android, Play Games can save your progress in your own Google account so you can continue on another device.
