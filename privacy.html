Privacy policy

App policy last updated: September 23, 2026 · Website hosting information added: October 25, 2026

Good Owl Apps does not store nor asks you for any personal information. Data is only stored locally in private storage on your device.

Good Owl Apps does not collect data for analytics, crash reporting, screen tracking or advertising, and does not share data with third parties.

Cloud backup in “Logic Owl”

The app “Logic Owl” (German: “Logik-Eule”) offers an optional cloud backup of your puzzle progress: which puzzles you have solved, your times and your current boards. The backup is stored in your own account with Google or Apple, so that you can continue on another device. Good Owl Apps has no access to these accounts and does not receive a copy of this data.

Android: Google Play Games Services

If you enable “Sync with Play Games” in the settings and are signed in to Google Play Games, your progress is stored in your own Google account using the Saved Games feature.

While you are signed in, the app reads your Play Games player name in order to show it on the settings screen. It is not stored, transmitted or used for anything else.

Sign-in and data transfer are handled entirely by Google Play services – the Android app itself has no internet permission. How Google processes this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

iPhone and iPad: Apple iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the app stores your progress in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account (CloudKit). Only you can access this data; Good Owl Apps cannot see it. The app does not read your name, e-mail address or any other account details.

“Sync with iCloud” is switched on by default and only works while you are signed in to iCloud. The app connects to the internet only for this backup. How Apple processes this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Your choices

The cloud backup is optional. You can switch it off at any time in the settings (“Sync with Play Games” on Android, “Sync with iCloud” on iPhone and iPad). “Delete all progress” removes your data on the device and in the cloud.

“Export progress” saves your progress to a file at a location you choose. Good Owl Apps does not receive this file.
